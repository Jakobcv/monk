## Problem
Two people shopping from one Larder list each see it as it was when they opened it. A tick on one phone doesn't reach the other until the app is reopened, so items are bought twice or missed, and households give up on sharing the list within a month.

## Goals
- A tick on one phone shows on the other within two seconds while both have signal.
- A tick made without signal reaches the other phone as soon as signal returns, without the shopper doing anything.
- A shopper can tell an item the other person just ticked from one ticked earlier.

## Non-goals
- Editing an item's text from two phones at once. Ticks are what change mid-shop; text is written at home.
- Showing where the other shopper is in the shop.
- Any change to how a list is shared or who can see it.
