# Household 3 — accompanied shop

Two adults, one teenager. Shared a list for five weeks, then stopped.

- They split up at the entrance: one to fresh food, one to the freezers.
- Near the freezers the second phone showed the list as it was at the door for four minutes.
- Oat milk was ticked on the first phone at 10:12 and bought again by the second at 10:16.
- On the way home: "This is why we just text each other now."
- The teenager had added "a treat" to the list. Nobody knew who.
