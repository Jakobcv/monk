A shop is the worst connection in a household's week, so the shopping view never depends on one.

- Anything done in the shopping view is kept on the phone first and sent when it can be.
- Nothing waiting to send is shown as an error. After a minute unsent, say so once, quietly, at the foot of the list.
- Sending happens in the order things were done, so an untick sent after its tick undoes it.
