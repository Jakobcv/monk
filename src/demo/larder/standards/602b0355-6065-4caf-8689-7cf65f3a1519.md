- Name things the way a household does: a list, the shop, the plan. Never an "entity" or a "workspace".
- Buttons say what happens: Send to list, not Continue.
- Use first names. Larder is used between people who know each other.
- No exclamation marks, and nothing that congratulates someone for shopping.
