Everything in the shopping view has to work one-handed at arm's length.

- Targets are at least 44 px, with nothing important in the top third of the screen.
- Nothing needs a swipe without a tap that does the same thing.
- Text stays readable at the largest system text size, and the list reflows rather than truncating.
- A ticked item is struck through **and** moved, never shown by colour alone.
