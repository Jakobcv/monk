## Problem
A list made from a meal plan carries every ingredient, including the salt, oil and rice a household always has. People delete those lines by hand, or stop reading the list closely enough to notice what's missing; 48% of planners say they don't want staples listed at all.

## Goals
- A household can say once which staples it always has.
- A list made from a plan leaves those off.

## Non-goals
- Tracking how much of anything is in the cupboard.
- Staples on items typed by hand.
