## Problem
The Plus page lists features in a table and most visitors leave within ten seconds of reaching it. The one thing trial households said they'd pay for — fewer shops — is not on the page at all.

## Goals
- A household sees what Plus would save it, in its own shops, before the price.

## Non-goals
- Changing the price or what Plus includes.
- Anything shown to a household with less than a month of shopping in Larder.
