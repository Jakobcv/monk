Every study has a research plan before the first interview, with the questions it has to answer written down.

Notes go in as signals the same day, while they are still exact — one observation each, in the participant's words where possible. Insights are formed on the plan's Analysis board, from signals, and only when at least three signals point the same way.

A study is done when every research question has an insight or has been set aside on purpose.
