From the accompanied shops in Why shared lists get abandoned:

- A weekly shop takes about twenty minutes and the list is opened once, at the door, and then read without being closed.
- When two people shop together they split up at the entrance and meet at the till. The median gap between their ticks is eleven minutes.
- The list is read at arm's length with one hand; the other hand is on a trolley or a basket.
- Signal is worst at the back of the shop — freezers, drinks — and can drop for several minutes.
- People walk the same route every week.
