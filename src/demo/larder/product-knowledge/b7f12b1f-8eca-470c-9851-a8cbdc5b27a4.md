Most active households have two adults; about a third also have children, and in a tenth of those a teenager adds to the list.

- **The planner** writes most of the list and the meal plan, usually on a Sunday, at home.
- **The second shopper** mostly reads the list, in the shop, often on a weaker phone and a worse connection.
- **The contributor** — a teenager, a flatmate — adds items and never shops.

Households that shop together, side by side, have the fewest problems with a shared list and are the least representative of how it is used.
