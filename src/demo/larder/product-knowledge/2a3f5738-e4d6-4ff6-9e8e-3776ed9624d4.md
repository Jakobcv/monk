Larder is a shared grocery list and meal planner for households. A household is the unit: one list, shared by everyone who shops for it, and one week's meal plan that feeds it.

It is free. A paid tier, Larder Plus, has been trialled and is paused.

Three things matter more than anything else Larder does:

1. **The list is right in the shop.** Everything else is in service of the twenty minutes someone spends reading it in an aisle.
2. **Two people can use it at once.** Most households have two adults who shop, often at the same time.
3. **Planning feeds the list.** A plan that doesn't become a list is a plan nobody shops from.
