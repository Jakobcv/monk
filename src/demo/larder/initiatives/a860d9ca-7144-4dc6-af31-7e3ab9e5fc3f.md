People who plan the week's meals in Larder then copy every ingredient onto the list by hand, and most of them stop planning because of it: only 12% of households that make a plan add any of its ingredients to a list the same week. This initiative turns a plan into a list in one step, and leaves off what the household already has.

The boundary: from a plan to a list. Recipes themselves, and anything about cooking, are out. The research behind it is What stops a meal plan becoming a list.
