Larder is free, and a paid tier has been tried once: of 40 households offered a trial of Plus, 3 converted. This initiative is to find what a household would pay for and sell that.

Paused until shared lists keep households past their first month, because a paid tier sold to households who then stop using Larder is revenue that leaves with them. The study Would households pay for Larder Plus? carries on in the meantime.
