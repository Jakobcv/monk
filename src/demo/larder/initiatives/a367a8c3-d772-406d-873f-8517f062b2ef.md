A household that signs up and doesn't shop with Larder in its first week almost never comes back, and most new households stalled on an empty list with nobody else in it. This initiative got a new household from sign-up to a first shop: a list that starts with something on it, and the other adult invited before the first shop rather than after.

The boundary was the first week only. What happens once two people are sharing a list is its own initiative, Lists two people can shop from.
