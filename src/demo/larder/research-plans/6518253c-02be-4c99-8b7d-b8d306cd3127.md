## Problem statement

Plus converted 3 of 40 trial households. We don't know whether that is the price, the product, or the way it is sold, and the initiative is paused until we do.

## Background

Plus bundles meal plans without limits, recipe import and pantry tracking at a monthly price. The Plus page lists those features in a table.

## Approach

Five interviews with trial households, both those who paid and those who didn't, and a look at how visitors move through the Plus page.

## Participants

Five households from the Plus trial: two who kept paying, three who didn't.

## Discussion guide

1. What made you try Plus?
2. What did you use in it?
3. What would have made it worth paying for?
4. Who in your household decided?
