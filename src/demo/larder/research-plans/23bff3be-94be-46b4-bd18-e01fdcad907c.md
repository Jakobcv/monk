## Problem statement

Shared lists are abandoned at twice the rate of personal ones in their first month, and we don't know what goes wrong between two people using one list — so we can't tell whether the fix is in the list, in the invite, or somewhere else entirely.

## Background

Sharing is the most requested feature Larder has and its weakest for retention. Support hears about lists "not updating", but those tickets come from people still using the app, not from the ones who left.

## Approach

Eight interviews with households that shared a list for at least two weeks and then stopped, each walking through their last shop together, followed by a shop with one household where we watched both phones. Event data to size what we heard.

## Participants

Eight two-adult households who shared a list for at least two weeks and then stopped within 60 days, recruited from the in-app survey. Two with teenagers who add to the list.

## Discussion guide

1. Tell me about the last big shop you did.
2. Who wrote the list, and who shopped from it?
3. What happened when you were both in the shop?
4. When did you stop sharing the list, and what happened just before?
5. What do you use instead now?
