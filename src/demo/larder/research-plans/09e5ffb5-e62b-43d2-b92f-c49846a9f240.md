## Problem statement

People who plan meals in Larder mostly don't shop from the plan, and we don't know where the plan stops being useful on the way to the shop.

## Background

Planning weeks are Larder's best weeks — households open the app far more often — but only 12% of plans turn into a list the same week, and support keeps being asked how to send a recipe's ingredients to the list.

## Approach

Six interviews at the kitchen table while the household planned its week, then a survey of 400 planners to size what we heard.

## Participants

Six households that made at least three meal plans in the last two months, half of whom never added a plan's ingredients to a list. The survey went to everyone who planned in the last 60 days.

## Discussion guide

1. Show me how you decided what to eat this week.
2. Where does that go once it's decided?
3. Walk me through writing the list from it.
4. What did you leave off the list, and why?
5. When do you not bother planning?
