## Solution

On a shared list, an item someone else added carries their first name after it, in the secondary text colour. Your own items carry nothing. Pressing and holding an item someone else added offers "Ask about this", which opens a message to them with the item already named.

## Decisions

- Names only on other people's items — because on a two-person list your own name on half the items is noise.
- Asking opens the phone's own messages rather than a thread in Larder — because people already text each other about the list, and a second place to talk is a second place to miss a reply.

## Notes

Result: shipped to everyone twelve days ago. Every criterion was checked on a three-member test list and on a one-member list. Shared lists still used by both members after 30 days have moved from 38% to 44% since, though the live ticks behind a flag may account for some of that.
