## Problem
On a shared list nobody can tell who added an item, so an unfamiliar one gets bought on a guess or skipped on a guess, and teenagers' jokes get bought. People text each other to ask, which is the thing a shared list was meant to replace.

## Goals
- Anyone on a shared list can see who added an item without opening it.
- Someone can ask about an item from the item itself.

## Non-goals
- Comments or a conversation on an item.
- Who ticked an item. That is in Ticks reach the other phone while shopping.
- Any of this on a list with one member.
