## Problem
New households almost always have two adults who shop, but the invite lived in settings and was found, on average, eleven days after sign-up — long after the first shop, when a list shared late had already been replaced by texting.

## Goals
- A new household is offered the invite during setup, before its first shop.
- The invite can be skipped without losing anything.

## Non-goals
- Inviting more than one person during setup.
- Changing how an invite is accepted.
