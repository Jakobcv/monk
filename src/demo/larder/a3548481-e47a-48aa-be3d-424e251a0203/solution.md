## Solution

Setup's second step, straight after naming the list, asks who else shops for the household and offers to send them an invite. It can be skipped, and the invite stays where it was in settings.

## Result

Setup now asks, straight after naming the list, who else shops for the household, and sends that person an invite there and then. It can be skipped, and the invite is still in settings for later. Before, the invite was found on average eleven days after sign-up, long after the first shop.

How we know:
- In a new account, setup's second step offered the invite; skipping it finished setup and left the invite in settings.
- An invite sent during setup was accepted, and the other person saw the same list.
- In the 45 days since it shipped, new households with a second member within a week went from 18% to 33%.

Not checked: an invite to someone who already has a Larder list of their own.

## Decisions

- Offered, not required — because requiring it lost a third of sign-ups in the test.
