## Solution

Clear list empties the list at once and shows Undo at the foot of the screen for ten seconds. Undo puts every item back as it was.

## Result

Clearing a list can be undone. Clear list still empties it at once, but Undo shows at the foot of the screen for ten seconds and puts every item back as it was, ticked or not, on both phones.

How we know:
- A list of ticked and unticked items was cleared, then restored with Undo, every item as it was.
- Cleared and undone on one phone, the items came back on the other member's phone too.
- In the six weeks since it shipped, 55 days ago, support tickets about a cleared list went from about two a week to none.

Not checked: undoing while one of the phones has no signal.

## Decisions

- Undo rather than a confirmation — because a confirmation is tapped through as fast as the mistake it guards.
