## Solution

Clear list empties the list at once and shows Undo at the foot of the screen for ten seconds. Undo puts every item back as it was.

## Decisions

- Undo rather than a confirmation — because a confirmation is tapped through as fast as the mistake it guards.

## Notes

Result: shipped 55 days ago. Support tickets about a cleared list went from about two a week to none in the six weeks since.
