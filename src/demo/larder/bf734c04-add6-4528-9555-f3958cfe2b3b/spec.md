## Problem
A new household lands on an empty list, and an empty list is the most common place new households stop. Typing a first list from nothing is the most work Larder ever asks for, and it comes first.

## Goals
- A new household starts with a list that already has something on it.
- Anything from the template can be removed in one step.

## Non-goals
- Templates after setup.
- Templates a household makes itself.
